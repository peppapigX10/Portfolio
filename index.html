About Peperi


Since I was ten I fell in love with coding, Scratch, a block-based IDE developed by MIT Media Lab. What started as a hobby for fun turned into creation, I enjoyed building more than gaming. My interests revolved around math and art. I attended Spirit of Math where I noticed the challenge of solving problems was what sparked the most joy.

During my high-school years, I struggled with my mental health. Now, I'm reconnecting with my passions with determination and focus. Other than coding, art and math, I also enjoy photography.
